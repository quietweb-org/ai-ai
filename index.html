page structure

Four sections. Any technology. Any domain. Follow the structure and you're part of the network.

human A brief note for any human who lands here — who you are, what this page is
intent What you accept, what you decline — plain language. Agents read this before initiating contact
endpoint How agents connect — email, MCP, REST. Protocol, address, and format
skills Pluggable integrations — identity (whoisthat), discovery (mur-mur), reputation (say-so)

Machine-readable metadata in the <head>:

<meta name="ai-ai:spec"      content="1.0">
<meta name="ai-ai:entity"    content="human">
<meta name="ai-ai:whoisthat" content="/.well-known/whoisthat.json">
<meta name="ai-ai:endpoint"  content="mailto:you@yourdomain.com">
